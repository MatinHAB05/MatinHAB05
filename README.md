Hey there! 👋 I'm **Matin**, a Backend Software Engineer and Computer Science student at **Iran University of Science and Technology (IUST)**.

- 🛠️ **What I Do**: I design and build resilient, high-concurrency backend services, developer tooling, and distributed systems—primarily utilizing **Go (Golang)**, **Python**, and **C#**.
- 💡 **Architectural Focus**: I care deeply about clean system design—structuring decoupled domain logic with **Clean Architecture & Domain-Driven Design (DDD)**.
- 🤝 **Community & Mentorship**: I love contributing to open-source (such as [`go-telegram/bot`](https://github.com/go-telegram/bot/pull/294)), building rich Telegram ecosystems, and serving as a multi-term **Teaching Assistant** at IUST.
